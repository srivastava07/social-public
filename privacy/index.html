Privacy Policy

Last updated 23 September 2026

This policy explains how kathaa.toons (“the Service”) handles information. By using the Service you agree to the practices described here.

Information we collect

The Service collects only what is necessary to publish content to the social media accounts you authorise:

  • account identifiers for the pages, profiles and channels you connect
  • access credentials issued by those platforms when you grant permission
  • the content you choose to publish, and its captions

We do not collect personal messages, contacts, friend lists, or any information about people other than the account holder.

How we use it

Information is used solely to publish content to the accounts you have connected, and to show you the status of that content. It is not used for any other purpose.

Sharing

We do not sell, rent, or trade information with anyone. Information is shared only with the platform you are publishing to, and only to the extent publishing requires. Those platforms handle it under their own policies:

  • Meta Privacy Policy
  • Google Privacy Policy

Advertising and analytics

The Service does not serve advertising, does not use third-party analytics, and does not build profiles or track behaviour.

Retention

Access credentials are retained only while your account remains connected. When you disconnect an account or revoke permission, the associated credentials cease to be valid and are removed.

Security

Access credentials are treated as confidential, are not disclosed to third parties, and are protected by reasonable measures appropriate to their sensitivity. No method of storage or transmission is completely secure, and absolute security cannot be guaranteed.

Your choices

You may disconnect any account at any time, which immediately ends the Service’s access to it. See Data Deletion for instructions and for how to request removal.

Children

The Service is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect information from them.

Changes

This policy may be updated from time to time. The current version is always the one published at this address, with the date shown above.

Contact

Questions about this policy, and requests concerning your information, may be submitted through our contact page. Requests are answered within 30 days.
