Privacy Policy

Last updated 23 September 2026

This policy covers the Meta application kathaa.toons (App ID 2252634102251757) and the publishing tool it is used with.

In short: this application is a private publishing tool. It is operated by one person, it publishes that person's own videos to social media accounts they own, and it has no users other than its operator. It collects nothing from anyone else.

What the application does

The operator writes and renders short videos on their own computer. This application takes a finished video and uploads it to Facebook Pages, Instagram accounts and YouTube channels that the operator administers.

It is not a service offered to the public. No one else can sign in to it, and it does not act on behalf of anyone but its operator.

What information is accessed

When the operator signs in with Facebook or Google, the application receives and uses only what publishing requires:

  • the list of Facebook Pages the operator administers, and an access token for the chosen Page
  • the identifier of the Instagram professional account linked to that Page
  • a YouTube channel authorisation token
  • the operator's own name and account identifier, as returned with those tokens

No content, messages, friend lists, contacts, or information about any other person is requested or received.

Where it is stored

Access tokens and account identifiers are stored in a database file on the operator's own computer. They are not uploaded to any server, and there is no hosted backend to upload them to.

Videos are sent directly to Meta and Google when the operator publishes one, over their own APIs. Nothing is sent anywhere else.

What is not done

  • No information is sold, rented, or shared with any third party.
  • There is no advertising, no tracking, and no analytics.
  • No profiles are built and nothing is used for any purpose beyond publishing a video.

Third parties

Publishing necessarily sends the video and its caption to the platform it is being published to. Those platforms handle that content under their own policies:

  • Meta Privacy Policy — Facebook and Instagram
  • Google Privacy Policy — YouTube

Retention and deletion

Tokens are kept only while the operator is using the application, and are deleted whenever the operator removes them or revokes access. See Data Deletion for how.

Children

This application is not directed at children and has no users other than its operator.
