Data Deletion

Last updated 23 September 2026

How to end kathaa.toons’ access to your accounts and have any associated information removed.

Disconnect an account

You can revoke access at any time from the platform itself. Doing so immediately invalidates the credentials held for that account.

  • Facebook and Instagram — open Settings → Business integrations, select kathaa.toons, and choose Remove.
  • Google and YouTube — open Google Account → Third-party apps & services, select the app, and choose Remove access.

Request removal

Disconnecting an account, as above, is the fastest and most complete way to end access and have the associated credentials removed — it takes effect immediately and needs no request.

If you would like to make a request in writing instead, use the contact details in our Privacy Policy. Requests are completed within 30 days, and confirmation is sent once the deletion is done.

Content already published

Content that has been published is an ordinary post on the page, profile or channel it was published to, and belongs to that account. It is not removed by disconnecting or by a deletion request, and should be deleted from the platform directly.
